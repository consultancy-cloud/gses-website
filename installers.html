Why work with us

Not a labour-hire list. A working inspectorate.

Plenty of companies will add your name to a spreadsheet and call you when something turns up. GSES is different for a simple reason: we hold the contracts. We have been the Clean Energy Regulator's approved inspection contractor since 2011, the official inspectorate for the Queensland Battery Booster Program, and the delivery partner on state solar, housing and EV charger inspection programs.

That means the work is real, ongoing and scheduled — not speculative. It also means we know exactly what a good inspection looks like, because we helped the regulator design the scheme in the first place, and we train the industry through our own RTO.

Eligibility

What you need to join the network.

Inspection work carries a legal weight that installation work does not. Our inspectors sign findings that regulators, insurers and tribunals rely on, so the entry bar is deliberately firm — and applies equally to everyone.

The critical one is independence. Under the CER scheme an inspector must be independent of the installation being inspected and hold an unrestricted electrical licence. If you install in a region, you will not be dispatched to inspect your own work or your competitors' where a conflict exists.

New to inspection but a strong installer? Apply anyway. Several of our best inspectors came across from the tools, and the application asks about your experience level precisely so we can match you to the right program and support.

What we handle

You inspect. We do the rest.

Consent and scheduling

We obtain homeowner consent, make contact, book the appointment and manage every reschedule and no-show. You get a confirmed time and address.

Checklists that match the scheme

Program-specific checklists built by the engineers who negotiate the scope with the client, so what you capture on site is exactly what the report needs.

Report writing and QA

Your findings and photos go to our engineering team for review and report production. You are not writing formal reports at 9pm.

The regulator relationship

Submissions to the CER portal, installer right-of-reply correspondence and program reporting are all handled by GSES. You never deal with the paperwork chain.

Technical backup

Ambiguous defect, unusual configuration, unsafe site? Call the engineering team from the roof. We would far rather answer the question than fix a wrong finding later.

Straightforward payment

Contractor engagement with clear per-job rates agreed upfront and invoicing run through the same system that dispatches your work.
