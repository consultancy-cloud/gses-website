SERVICE 01

Compliance & QA inspections

Independent field verification against Australian Standards, scheme rules, WHS obligations, manufacturer instructions and the client's own specification — whichever of those the asset is answerable to.

This is our largest practice by volume and the reason regulators and state governments appoint us: findings that hold up because the firm producing them has nothing to gain from the outcome.

How we work

The same four disciplines, every engagement.

01

Independence first

No products, no installation arm, no referral fees, no stake in the outcome. Where a program requires it, inspector independence from the installation is enforced at allocation.

02

Measured, not estimated

Thermography, curve tracing, pyranometry and logged data. Where a finding can be measured we measure it, and the instrument and method are stated in the report.

03

Written to a clause

Every finding cites the standard, scheme rule, manufacturer instruction or contract term it breaches, with photographic evidence and a severity rating.

04

Defensible afterwards

Reports are built to survive scrutiny from a regulator, an insurer or a tribunal — and our engineers appear as expert witnesses when they have to.
