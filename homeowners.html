What we resolve

The problems an inspection actually solves.

The situations homeowners bring us most often. If yours isn't here, it is probably a variation of one of them — ask us and we'll say honestly whether an inspection helps.

Performance

Your bill didn't drop

Undersized inverter, strings on the wrong MPPT, unmodelled shading, or a quoted yield that was never achievable.

We: measure actual output against what the site can genuinely produce.
Faults

Inverter errors and dead strings

Repeat fault codes or a section producing nothing — usually an isolator, connector or earthing fault, not the inverter you're told to replace.

We: trace the fault to the actual component, so you replace the right thing once.
Safety

Water in the DC isolator

One of the most frequent defects found nationally. A failed IP rating corrodes the switch and creates a fire risk while the system keeps producing.

We: open and inspect every isolation point, and rate the urgency.
Compliance

Undersized protection, missing earthing

DC breaker sizing and equipotential bonding are two of the defects we report most. Both are invisible from the ground and neither stops the system generating.

We: verify protection sizing and earthing continuity against AS/NZS 5033.
Battery

Is my battery safe where it is?

AS/NZS 5139 sets hard rules on location, clearances, fire separation and signage. Batteries beside exit paths or on bedroom walls are not edge cases.

We: assess siting and protection against the standard GSES helped author.
Disputes

Warranty claims and defect notices

The installer has gone quiet, or a regulator has flagged your system and you don't know how serious it is or whether the quoted fix is fair.

We: produce standards-referenced evidence an insurer or tribunal will accept.
Property

Buying or selling with solar

A ten-year-old array with no paperwork is a liability, not a feature — and a building inspector will not open a DC isolator.

We: assess condition and compliance so the asset can be priced honestly.
EV

Your EV charger trips the house

Chargers installed on circuits that can't carry them, without load management or the protection the network required.

We: verify the installation, protection and supply capacity.
Paperwork

No documentation for what you paid for

No compliance certificate, no shutdown procedure, no as-built diagram. That paperwork is a legal requirement — and the first thing every insurer asks for.

We: audit what exists and document what the installed system actually is.
The inspection

What we actually look at.

Where we look, and in what order
Every numbered point below is inspected, tested and documented.
DC — array to inverter and battery AC — inverter to switchboard and grid Documentation is checked against every point on this line.
01

Array & mounting

  • Module condition, damage and degradation
  • Rail, clamp and fixing integrity
  • Roof penetrations and weatherproofing
  • Orientation, tilt and shading against yield
  • Earthing and equipotential bonding
02

DC side

  • Isolator enclosures, IP rating and water ingress
  • DC protection device rating and sizing
  • Cable selection, support and UV exposure
  • Connector type-matching and terminations
  • String configuration and polarity
03

Inverter & switchboard

  • Inverter mounting, ventilation and fault history
  • AS/NZS 4777 grid settings and export limits
  • Switchboard labelling and signage
  • AC protection, RCDs and main switch arrangement
  • Metering and monitoring configuration
04

Battery storage

  • Location, clearances and fire separation
  • Restricted-location and habitable-room rules
  • Ventilation, enclosure and mounting
  • Protection, isolation and emergency shutdown
  • Signage and shutdown procedure on site
05

Performance

  • Actual generation against realistic site yield
  • String-level output comparison
  • Thermal imaging of modules and connections
  • Monitoring data review where available
  • Underperformance root-cause assessment
06

Documentation

  • Certificate of Compliance / electrical safety certificate
  • As-installed system diagram
  • Commissioning and test records
  • Shutdown procedure and emergency information
  • Warranty documents and installer details

Straight answers

Do you fix what you find?

No — deliberately. The moment we could profit from the rectification, our findings stop being independent. We tell you exactly what needs doing so you can get competitive quotes, and we'll re-inspect to confirm it was done properly.

What does an inspection cost?

It depends on scope and location. Tell us the situation and we'll give you a fixed price before anyone is booked. No charge for the conversation.

Will you inspect another company's work?

That is essentially all we do. Regulators and governments engage us specifically to assess other companies' work. Your installer's identity changes nothing about the report.

Do I need to be home?

Yes, or someone over 18 who can give access to the roof, switchboard, inverter and any battery. We'll shut the system down briefly to test it safely.

What should I have ready?

The quote and invoice, the compliance certificate, any system diagram, monitoring logins and warranty documents. If you have none of it, that's a finding in itself.

Can I use the report in a dispute?

Yes. Findings cite the specific standard or requirement breached, with photographic evidence. Our engineers also provide expert witness services where a matter escalates.

How quickly can you get here?

We cover every state and territory, so lead time depends on location and urgency. If it sounds like an immediate hazard, say so on the call — and shut the system down at the isolator meanwhile.
